## Effective permissions and capabilities

- iOS: APNs entitlement, photo-library purpose string, custom URL schemes. No camera, microphone, Face ID, location, associated domains, Sign in with Apple, app groups, keychain groups, or background modes were added.
- Actual versionCode 4 merged permissions: network state/Internet; notification, wake/reboot/FCM and launcher-badge capabilities; legacy storage capped at API 32; app-scoped receiver permission; Play install-referrer service binding; and unused biometric/fingerprint declarations. Final source additionally removes legacy storage, biometric, camera, microphone and overlay permissions. It also removes unused camera/video/document-tree visibility intents while keeping HTTPS, gallery and share intents.
- Exported components in the actual AAB: `MainActivity`; FCM's receiver protected by Google's send permission; AndroidX profile installer receiver protected by the system `DUMP` permission. Other app providers, activities, services and notification receivers are not exported. No broad `QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES` or unrestricted exported service exists.
- Android backup remains enabled for nonsensitive preferences, while Expo SecureStore preferences are excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.
